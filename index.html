THE BIG IDEA

What are
past modals?

We use them to talk about things that happened—or may have happened—in the past.

🤔

Possibilities

Maybe it happened.

💭

Regrets

I wish I had acted differently.

🕵️

Evidence helps me guess.

02 / 08 · THE IDEA
1 · REGRET

Should have

For something it was better to do, but we didn’t do it.

Subjectshould havepast participle
I should have studied! 📚
Debí haber estudiado.
I should have taken an umbrella. ☔
Debí haber llevado un paraguas.
Oops… next time! 😅
03 / 08 · SHOULD HAVE
2 · OPPORTUNITY

Could have

A past possibility or an opportunity we didn’t take.

Subjectcould havepast participle
I could have taken the earlier bus. 🚌
Podría haber tomado el bus anterior.
We could have finished yesterday. ✅
Podríamos haber terminado ayer.
So many possibilities! 🚏
04 / 08 · COULD HAVE
3 · MAYBE

Might have
/ May have

Use these when we are not sure what happened.

Subjectmight / may havepast participle
She might have missed the bus. 🚌
Puede que haya perdido el bus.
He may have forgotten my message. 📱
Puede que haya olvidado mi mensaje.
We’re not 100% sure! 🤷
05 / 08 · MIGHT / MAY HAVE
4 · DETECTIVE MODE

Must have

A strong conclusion based on evidence. It is not an obligation here!

Subjectmust havepast participle
The streets are wet. It must have rained. 🌧️
Las calles están mojadas. Debió de llover.
She got the best grade. She must have studied! 🏆
Obtuvo la mejor nota. ¡Debió de estudiar!
Clue + logic = deduction 🕵️
06 / 08 · MUST HAVE
5 · CHECK YOURSELF

Spot the mistake!

❌ Incorrect

She should has studied.

I should have went.

✅ Correct

She should have studied.

I should have gone.

🎯 Quick challenge

I failed the exam. I _____ studied more.

Remember: have + past participle! ✍️
